Club Committee

Meet the team running Wallasey Chess Club

Our committee is made up of dedicated volunteers who work tirelessly to ensure the smooth running of the club and to provide the best possible experience for all our members. Elected annually at our AGM, they bring a wealth of chess knowledge and organizational expertise to their roles.
